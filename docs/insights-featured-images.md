# News & Insights featured-image requirements

The blog now supports local featured images through `featuredImage` and
`featuredImageAlt`, with optional caption, credit, and source URL fields.
Until approved article-specific images are supplied, each post uses the branded
EaseIntoAI editorial fallback generated by `ArticleCover`.

Required production assets should be 16:9, at least 1600×900, optimized as WebP
or AVIF, and cleared for website and social-sharing use.

| Article slug                                    | Recommended image direction                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `announcing-ai-for-women-entrepreneurs-webinar` | Branded historical webinar graphic or approved event photograph from the July 22 session                             |
| `too-busy-to-learn-ai-women-business-owners`    | Woman business owner transitioning from full-time work to an evening business task                                   |
| `ai-literacy-framework-practical-guide`         | Branded educational diagram showing practical AI-literacy decisions                                                  |
| `ai-productivity-myth-vs-reality`               | Business owner reviewing and correcting an AI-assisted draft                                                         |
| `prompt-engineering-beginners-guide`            | Coach, consultant, or creator refining clear written instructions                                                    |
| `ai-tools-you-can-use-today`                    | Approved product-interface montage or human-centered tool-selection scene                                            |
| `webinar-3-recap-prompt-engineering`            | Approved Webinar 3 photograph or branded recap graphic                                                               |
| `webinar-1-recap-understand-ai`                 | Approved Webinar 1 photograph or branded recap graphic                                                               |
| `openai-gpt5-what-it-means`                     | Licensed OpenAI product interface or a branded explanatory diagram; verify primary sources before editorial revision |
| `stop-fearing-ai-start-learning`                | Diverse non-technical professional learning through a practical business example                                     |

Avoid robots, glowing brains, futuristic heads, circuits, holograms, neon
technology scenes, and repeated generic laptop photography.
